Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Flag That has no sign-up. Everything is saved on your device.

I got a new phone. Where did my progress go?
Progress lives on the device it was made on and does not sync. A new device starts fresh.

How do I reset my stats?
Open Settings and use the reset option. Deleting the app also removes all saved data.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?
Open Settings and toggle the reminder. You can also turn notifications off in your phone's system settings.

No sound or vibration?
Check the sound and haptics toggles in Settings, and make sure your phone is not on silent.

A flag or country name looks wrong.
Tell us which one. We fix these fast.
